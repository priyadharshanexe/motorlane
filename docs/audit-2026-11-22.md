# Motorlane audit, 22 November 2026

An audit of the whole application in three roles: developer (code review of every business
rule), tester (negative and edge-case tests against the API), and user (browser walkthroughs
as a new renter and a new owner, on desktop and phone width). Every fix below has a
regression test, and the full suite (161 Django tests on MySQL, plus the frontend checks)
passes in CI.

Severity: **High** means money or data could be wrong; **Medium** means a wrong result or a
weak guarantee under realistic use; **Low** means it is noticeable but harmless.

## User walkthrough (desktop 1440 px and phone 375 px)

A new renter (phone) and a new owner (desktop, then phone) signed up, verified their email,
listed a vehicle with a weekend rate, a deposit and a photo, searched, booked with times,
approved, paid in demo mode, picked up, returned (deposit refunded), reviewed and downloaded
the receipt. There was no horizontal overflow on any renter or owner page at 375 px, and no
console errors beyond the expected 401/400 responses of the sign-in flow.

Backend-caused issues found on the way were fixed above (A-05, A-06, A-08, A-10). Messages
that read well: unverified sign-in, invalid or expired verification link, reversed date range,
non-numeric price filter, cleaning-window conflicts, self-booking, the 90-day limit.

### UI issues for the redesign (not fixed here)

1. Sign-up with a malformed email or a short password relies on the browser's own popup; there is no inline message.
2. No "resend verification email" action on the sign-in page after "Check your inbox and verify your email".
3. Field errors are prefixed with the raw field name ("end date: …", "min price: …").
4. On phone width, the workspace tab bar (Overview, Saved vehicles, My bookings, Account…) is cut off at the right edge with no scroll hint.
5. The vehicle-type select in the catalog filter does not apply in some flows; the same filter works from the URL.
6. Checkout times use the native time input (15-minute steps); a slot picker that shows free times would be clearer.
7. The booking timeline labels the renter as "you or the renter"; it should know who is viewing.
8. Owner and renter booking lists are not paginated (fine at demo scale).
9. The "Skip to content" link becomes visible inside page content after programmatic focus changes.
